Student Information

You must complete a room inventory. Here’s how!

  1. Note any damaged, missing, or broken furniture in the Check-In Notes/Damage column. Not all furniture or items are found in every room, e.g. not every room has a hutch.
  2. Use only exact measurements/descriptions. E.g. “2-inch scratch,” “4 marks,” or “loose desk leg.” DO NOT USE inexact measurements/descriptions. E.g. “Huge scratch,” “Several marks,” or “broken desk leg.”
  3. Properly determine front, back, left, and right walls. To do so, put the entrance of the room at your back. That wall is now considered the BACK wall. The wall in front of you is the FRONT wall, to your left is the LEFT wall, and to your right is the RIGHT wall.
  4. Questions? Talk to your RA or your AC.
